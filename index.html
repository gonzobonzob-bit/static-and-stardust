The signal

Two things that never really left us.

Static & Stardust lives at the point where an old TV loses its signal and the sky shows through anyway — the fuzz of a rerun you've seen a hundred times, and the wide-open wonder of looking up at 2am.

We design apparel for people who quote the movie, remember the band, and still laugh at the meme a week later. No fast-fashion drops, no chasing trends we don't actually like — just graphics we'd wear ourselves, printed well and built to last past one wash.

Every design starts as a small batch. If it resonates, it stays; if it doesn't, we move on. That's the whole strategy.

We're a family collective at heart — a small circle of creators and suppliers who work together like family, not a faceless factory line.
